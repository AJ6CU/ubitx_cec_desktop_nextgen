SSB Demodulator: window stops responding to clicks after right-clicking NR

SDRangel version: 7.27.2-1315-gc43757c88 (Qt 6.11.1, arm64, macOS 26.6.2), GUI build


SUMMARY

After opening the FFT noise reduction dialog by right-clicking the NR button
of the SSB Demodulator and then closing it, the demodulator's window ignores
most mouse input: buttons such as NR and AGC do not change state, and
checkboxes and scrolling do not respond. The knobs still turn. One more right
click, released outside the demodulator, puts everything back to normal.

Only the mouse is involved -- no REST API, no other program. Audio and
everything else keep working throughout.


TO REPRODUCE

1. Open a device set with an SSB Demodulator.
2. Right-click NR in the SSB Demodulator window. The FFT Noise reduction
   dialog opens.
3. Close the dialog.
4. Click AGC or NR: neither changes state. Try a checkbox, or scrolling:
   no response. Turning a knob still works.
5. Right-click and release anywhere outside the demodulator.
6. Click AGC or NR again: they work.


EXPECTED

After the dialog closes, the demodulator's window responds to clicks as
normal.


ACTUAL

It ignores ordinary clicks, checkboxes and scrolling until another right
click is released outside it.


WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE

As if the window is still waiting for the release of the right click that
opened the dialog: the press went to the NR button, but the release went to
the dialog, so the button never saw it. The next right-click release, from
anywhere, seems to be what clears it. That is a guess from the behaviour, not
from reading the code.


QUESTION

Is this already known, or fixed in a later build?
